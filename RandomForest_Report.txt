  RESULT INTERPRETATION
  ────────────────────────────────────────
  • Test accuracy of 90.2% means the model correctly
    identifies the disease for ~9 in 10 unseen patients.
  
  • Precision 86.3%: when the model predicts a disease,
    it is correct 86.3% of the time (low false-positive
    rate — important to avoid unnecessary alarm).
  
  • Recall 90.2%: the model catches 90.2% of actual
    disease cases (low false-negative rate — important
    so real illnesses are not missed).
  
  • F1-score 87.6% is the harmonic mean of precision &
    recall, balancing both concerns.  A score above 85 %
    is considered strong for a 41-class classifier.
  
  • CV accuracy 85.2% ± 4.2% shows the result is
    stable across different train/test splits, not just
    one lucky draw.
  
  • Train accuracy is higher than test accuracy — this is
    EXPECTED and healthy.  A ~10% gap indicates the
    regularisation is working without underfitting.
  
  • WHY IT WAS 100 % BEFORE:
    The original code used a duplicated dataset (4 920
    rows, only 303 unique).  Identical rows leaked into
    both train and test, so the model memorised answers
    rather than learning patterns.
